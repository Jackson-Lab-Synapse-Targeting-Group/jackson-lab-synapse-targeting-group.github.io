Speculative Enquiries

We welcome enquiries from motivated scientists at any career stage — undergraduate project students, MSc students, postdocs, and independent fellowship applicants. If our research aligns with your interests and you would like to discuss opportunities, please get in touch.

When contacting us, please include a brief statement of your research interests, your CV, and (for fellowship applicants) an outline of the project you have in mind.
